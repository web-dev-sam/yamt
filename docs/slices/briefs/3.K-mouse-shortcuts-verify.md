   Steps and what to observe (paste what you saw — statuses/tints, the middle's text where it

   1. **Hint.** Hover a conflict's left apply icon and its ignore icon: read each `<title>`; a
      screenshot of the tooltip if it shows.
   2. **Ctrl+click on apply.** On a conflict: the middle holds that side's lines, both sides decided
      (no tint, no icons on either side). One Ctrl+Z: both sides wait again and the middle is back.
   3. **Ctrl+click on ignore.** On a conflict: the middle holds the other side's lines, both sides
      decided. Ctrl+Z.
   4. **Ctrl+right-click** on a waiting conflict's left text: left ignored, right still waiting,
      middle unchanged, no menu window, the left pane's selection unchanged. Repeat on a one-sided
      change: resolved whole. A plain right-click elsewhere: whatever happened before (say what).
   5. **Ctrl at drag release.** Drag from one apply icon over two conflicts with Ctrl held at the
      release: both conflicts fully decided; one Ctrl+Z restores both.
   6. **Same file both ways.** In `/tmp/yamt-test-msa`, resolve a text file with conflicts using
      only Ctrl+click / Ctrl+right-click; in `/tmp/yamt-test-msb`, make the same choices with plain
      icon clicks (apply one side, ignore the other); Mark resolved in both; `cmp` the two written
      files and paste the result.
   7. **Scrollbar press.** Click a left change (outline), press and drag the left pane's scrollbar:
      it scrolls; afterwards `document.activeElement` is the left `.cm-content` and ArrowDown moves
      the outline.
   8. **Wheel notch.** Pointer over the right pane: five Ctrl+wheel notches as one fast burst
      (`xdotool keydown ctrl click --repeat 5 --delay 30 5 keyup ctrl`): the outline moves five
      changes (or stops at the lowest), not two or three.
