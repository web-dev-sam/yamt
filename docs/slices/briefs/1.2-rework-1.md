## Change (findings, then what to do)

1. [should, correctness, CM12] `internal/scrollSync.ts:84` — `sync()` re-maps on any scroll event
   even when the pane's vertical `scrollTop` did not change: a horizontal scroll (lines do not
   wrap, so this is common), a CM horizontal `scrollIntoView`, or a late echo. When a follower
   is held at an insertion point (e.g. the right pane scrolled into vueuse remote lines
   98–106), such an event snaps the user's pane to the chunk's top or bottom — a visible jump.
   Fix: remember every pane's vertical `scrollTop` as it is right after a sync (read back inside
   the frame, after the writes); at the start of `sync()`, return early when the source pane's
   `scrollTop` still equals the remembered value. Smoke: repeat the feedback-loop check with the
   right pane scrolled into the middle of vueuse remote 98–106, plus one horizontal scroll of
   that pane; no pane's `scrollTop` changes over the next 5 frames.
2. [should, correctness] `internal/scrollSync.test.ts:57` — every test builds the content ends
   with the same per-pane offset as the last chunk bottom, so deleting the content-end anchor
   passes every test. Add a case where the tails differ (e.g. chunk bottom at (10,17,17) with
   ends (20,27,30)): `targets(anchors, "center", 27 * LINE_PX).right === 30 * LINE_PX`, and a
   point midway between the chunk bottom and the end interpolates.
3. [should, boundary] `internal/geometry.ts:42` — `chunkRows` zips MergeController's `#chunks`
   with the field's spans by index and throws inside the frame on a mismatch, which would
   silently stop all later painting and syncing. Fix: `ChunkSpan` carries the `chunk: Chunk`
   itself (set once when the field is created; mapping keeps it), so `chunkRows` maps one array
   and `#chunks`, the index lookup and the throw go away.
4. [should, boundary, TS11] `internal/tone.ts:7` — `tone.ts` exports four names again (`Tone`,
   `Side`, `sideChanged`, `chunkTone`), undoing rework 1.1's split. Fix: move `Side`,
   `CHANGED_SIDES` and `sideChanged` into `internal/side.ts`, imported by `tone.ts`,
   `decorations.ts` and `strips.ts`; `tone.ts` goes back to `Tone` + `chunkTone`.
5. [should, boundary] `internal/tone.ts:5` — `Pane` means `"local" | "center" | "remote"` in
   tone.ts and `"left" | "center" | "right"` in geometry.ts. Fix: delete tone.ts's private
   `Pane`; type `chunkTone`'s parameter as `Side | "center"`, so `Pane` has one meaning (the
   screen column in geometry.ts).
6. [nit, boundary] `internal/scrollSync.ts:66` — `#syncing` holds the follower panes whose next
   scroll is our own echo. Rename to `#echoes` and `#release` to `#clearEchoes`.
