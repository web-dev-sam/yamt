## Findings (verbatim) and decisions

1. #596 (boundary, should, CP1) `frontend/src/ui/MergeView.vue:118` — "Nothing at the section says
   why it rounds without `overflow-hidden` and why it carries `px-1 pb-1`. A reader who removes the
   'stray' inset, or adds `overflow-hidden` so the panes look rounded, silently brings back the
   WebKitGTK scroll slowdown or lets square pane corners poke past the arc." Fix: one HTML comment
   above the section in the strip comment's style, e.g. `<!-- Rounded without clipping: overflow
   hidden around the scrolling panes was slower in WebKitGTK (plan §2 #51); the 4 px inset keeps the
   pane corners inside the arc. -->`.
2. #597 (boundary, should, vision 2) — "The island geometry is copied as literals with nothing
   linking the copies: `rounded-[10px]` in FileList, MergeView and ChoiceView, and the 5 px gap as
   `1.25` in App (`p-1.25 gap-1.25`, twice), MergeView, ChoiceView and FileList's handle
   (`-right-1.25 w-1.25`). The handle only sits in the gap while its width equals App's gap."
   Decision (orchestrator, vision 2 over the brief's "no other token" line, which was about
   colours): add `--radius-island: 10px;` and `--spacing-island: 5px;` to `style.css`'s `@theme`
   and use `rounded-island`, `p-island` / `gap-island`, `-right-island w-island` everywhere the
   island radius or gap appears. The 6 px banner radius stays `rounded-md`; the 4 px non-clipping
   inset stays `px-1 pb-1` (it is derived from the radius, see the comment of finding 1).
3. #598 (boundary, nit) `frontend/src/ui/MergeToolbar.vue:59` — "`bg-window` on the toolbar and on
   the two placeholder headers (MergeView.vue:116, ChoiceView.vue:49) paints the frame colour again
   over the html background". Fix: drop `bg-window` from those three headers.
4. #599 (correctness, nit) `frontend/src/ui/FileList.vue:40` — "The scrolling `ul` reaches the
   aside's right edge and ends 4 px above its bottom. When many files make it scroll, the last 6 px
   of the scrollbar extend up to 2 px past the 10 px rounded corner." Fix: `mr-0.5` on the list.
