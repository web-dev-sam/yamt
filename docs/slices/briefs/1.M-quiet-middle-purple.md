# Slice 1.M — middle pane without word marks; conflicts purple

The owner, looking at a conflict (`language-tools-script-setup.ts`): the brighter word marks are
"chaotic and unhelpful". The middle pane marks the union of what either side changed, in one
colour, so nearly the whole block lights up and nothing says which side did what. The owner wants
word marks on the side panes only (left = local vs base, right = remote vs base). And: "a conflict
shows as red, which reads like something got deleted — confusing in WebStorm too; use purple for
conflicts". Decisions: plan §2 #31 (middle pane: no word marks) and #32 (purple). The engine
changes that follow (line-by-line pairing, removal points) are a later task; this one is the
editor and palette part only.

## Behaviour (what the user sees)

- The middle pane never shows word marks: not on an open block, not on a half-applied conflict,
  not after undo. Its block tint is unchanged.
- Left and right panes keep their word marks exactly as today (same ranges, same status rules).
- Conflicts are purple everywhere a conflict is drawn: block tint in all three panes, word marks on
  both side panes, the gutter/point markers, the connector bands in both strips. Today all of them
  read `--color-chunk-conflict` / `--color-chunk-conflict-word`, so the palette change should be
  enough; check each surface in the screenshot.
- Values: `--color-chunk-conflict: #8150be6b`, `--color-chunk-conflict-word: #8150bea6`
  (JetBrains New UI dark palette "Purple6" at the alphas the red had). Change them only if the
  screenshot shows text hard to read or marks not clearly brighter than the tint; report the final
  values either way.
