2. **#842 [should → promoted, boundary] `stripWaves.ts:32`** — "`StripWaves.paint` hand-writes the same

   not by concept. As a result two keyed path layers of one strip (bands and waves) now live in two files
   with two copies of the kept-node scheme (plan §2 #51), while the icon-row building (strips.ts:60-151),
   a separate concept, stayed." Fix: "Move the icon-row building (`APPLY_ICON`, `parsedIcon`,
   `iconElement`, `isCovered`, `actionButton`, `arrowRow`) to its own `stripIcons.ts`. Make `dropMissing`

   `#paintWaves` beside `#paintBands`, using it. Then delete stripWaves.ts." (Line numbers are round 1's;
   the panes rework may have moved them.)
