# 3.K — design: keyboard focus on a side and on a change (board "Keyboard focus" row 1)

Agent: `designer` (read-only). Output: 2–3 file-level options plus a recommendation (your output
schema). You design; the orchestrator chooses; one `web-impl` builds it afterwards inside
`frontend/src/editor/**` and `frontend/src/ui/**` (no Rust, no IPC change expected — say so if
your design needs one).

## The product rule (settled — `docs/plan.md` §2 #54; do not reopen)

The owner's own design (not WebStorm's), with their three answers folded in:

1. A click in a side pane (left = local, right = remote) focuses that side; a click on one of its
   change blocks focuses that change. Both are visible as an outline.
2. On a focused change: Enter/Space apply it; Backspace/Delete/x ignore that side of it (plan §2
   #33 semantics, exactly what the side's apply icon / × do); Down/Up move to the next/previous
   change **still waiting on that side** (`isSideOpen`, `editor/internal/status.ts`).
3. Left/Right move to the other side: to the other half of the same conflict when that half still
   waits; otherwise to the change last focused on that side (each side remembers one); otherwise
   the side alone. Left on the left side / Right on the right side: nothing.
4. A side alone (no change focused): Down focuses the first waiting change at or below the top of
   that pane's visible area, Up the last at or above the bottom; that side has none waiting → the
   same rule on the other side.
5. After an apply or ignore (by key), the focus moves to the next waiting change below on that
   side, else the top-most one still waiting there; none left on that side → the other side is
   focused with no change focused.
6. Ctrl/Cmd+scroll over a side focuses that side and its next (wheel down) / previous (wheel up)
   waiting change; from a side alone, the rule in 4 picks the first one.
7. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Y redo — anywhere in the window, not only in
   the middle pane.

Orchestrator defaults (also in #54; keep unless you find a concrete problem, then say so in
`open_questions` as a user-visible consequence):

- Decided sides are not stops (a handled side loses its marks, #33).
- Down on the lowest waiting change and Up on the top-most stay put (no wrap).
- A newly focused change is scrolled into view; the other panes follow through the existing sync.
- The middle keeps its typing keys; it is not reachable with Left/Right; a click in it removes the
  side outline (each side's remembered change stays).
- Ctrl/Cmd+scroll over the middle scrolls as usual.
- Undo/redo leave the focus where it is. A focused change decided any other way (icons, drag,
  Ctrl/Cmd+click, "apply all non-conflicting", redo, a remembered change decided from the other
  side) moves on by rule 5 the next time it matters — say exactly when.
- Inside a find box (Ctrl/Cmd+F, #44) undo stays the box's own; the box's Enter stays "next
  match".
- Ctrl/Cmd+click (#46), drag over icons (#45) and the icons keep working.
- Arrow keys and Space no longer scroll a focused side; Page Up/Down and the wheel still do.
- A file opened read-only (a resolved file, #37) takes no apply/ignore keys.

## Facts you need (read in the tree, 2026-10-12, HEAD `0b74b20`)

- Side panes: `EditorState.readOnly` + `EditorView.editable.of(false)` + `tabindex="0"` on the
  content (`frontend/src/editor/internal/pane.ts:117-124`), no keymap. The middle has
  `defaultKeymap`, `historyKeymap`, `indentWithTab` (`pane.ts:107-115`); its history is the only
  undo history (`MergeController.undo/redo` → `@codemirror/commands` on the center view).
- Statuses live in the center's `chunkField` (`editor/internal/chunkField.ts`); each side mirrors
  them in `sideStatusField` via the `showStatuses` effect (`editor/index.ts:205-216`,
  `internal/sideStatus.ts`). `openBlockAt` (`internal/modClick.ts`) finds the open block of a side
  at a document position.
- `MergeController.#act` dispatches `actionSteps` and then **focuses the center**
  (`editor/index.ts:197-203`) — today every apply/ignore moves keyboard focus to the middle.
- Geometry: `chunkRows` (`internal/geometry.ts`), `Layout` cache dropped by `rowsChanged`;
  `ScrollSync` (`internal/scrollSync.ts`) aligns panes by anchors; plan §2 #51 forbids geometry
  work per scroll frame. `#visibleLines()` computes the center's visible lines only.
- Find: `internal/find.ts`, keymap scope `"editor search-panel"`, one bar per pane.
- Every opened file keeps its own hidden `MergeView` with its own editors and toolbar
  (`ui/MergeView.vue`; inactive = `invisible [content-visibility:hidden]`, #51). A window-level key
  listener must act on the shown file only (#579: a first-match selector or a listener per view hit
  the wrong file).
- `ui/useMergeController.ts` is the only `ui → editor` import; `MergeToolbar.vue` emits
  undo/redo; `MergeController`'s public surface is listed in `docs/architecture.md` §5.2 — any
  addition is a surface change you must name.
- Read-only views pass `{ readOnly: true }` (no strip presses, no mod-click).

## What each option must answer

1. **State.** Where the focused side, the focused change and each side's remembered change live
   (a CodeMirror field per side view, the controller, or `ui/`), keyed how (`ChunkId`?), and how
   they stay correct when statuses change, the middle is edited, or undo reopens a change. One
   owner per fact (vision 2).
2. **Navigation rules 2–6 as pure functions** over the chunk states and pane positions, with the
   on-screen rule (4) per side pane, conflicts present on both sides, and changes with zero lines
   on a side (a removal shown as a thin bar).
3. **Keys.** How Enter/Space/Backspace/Delete/x/arrows bind on a side (keymap and its precedence,
   macOS Backspace), and why they cannot fire in the middle or a find box.
4. **Outline.** How the focused side and the focused change are drawn (including a zero-line
   change), from the existing palette (`frontend/src/styles` / theme tokens), without per-frame
   geometry (#51).
5. **Scroll.** How a focused change is brought into view and how that interacts with
   `ScrollSync` (no echo loops, no jump when already visible). Where WebStorm has the same
   interaction (moving to the next difference), follow its source and cite it (DR6).
6. **Ctrl/Cmd+wheel.** Where the handler sits, how one step is measured (mouse notch vs touchpad
   pixels, `deltaMode`), and that plain wheel scrolling is untouched.
7. **Undo anywhere.** Where the window-level listener lives, how it reaches only the shown file,
   and how it avoids double undo when the middle has focus and stealing undo from a find box.
8. **`#act` focus.** Keyboard decisions must keep focus on the side; say what icon clicks and
   Ctrl/Cmd+click do with focus afterwards (today: middle) and why.
9. **Tests.** `editor/` and `.vue` get no mutation testing here, so name the unit tests that would
   fail for each rule's plausible bug, and the WebKit steps (real pointer clicks and key presses at
   visible positions) that prove it in the app.
