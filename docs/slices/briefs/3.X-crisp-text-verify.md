5. Chromium (WebView2 stand-in) at device scale factor 1.5 with fontconfig rgba=rgb (`chromium/probe.mjs`): fringe in the

6. Scroll timing (`scroll.sh` = `.omp/run/scroll-timing.py`, scale 1, renderer.ts, all opened, middle, three runs) on the
