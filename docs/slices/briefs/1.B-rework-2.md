## Findings (verbatim) and decisions

### #247 block (correctness) and #244 should (boundary) — the bar paints over glyphs

> #247: The opaque bar and its 1 px `--color-editor` box-shadow sit in the `::after`, which paints
> above the glyphs, so 4 pixel columns centred on the character boundary are painted over the code.
> Every glyph next to a mid-line bar loses ink: zoom-C28-right.png `foo(|a|)` loses both edges of
> `a` and the inner edges of the parentheses, so `a` reads as a boxed or squeezed glyph even at 1x.
> C21 `x|` loses the x's right column and C27 `|foo` loses the f's left column … Fix: Stop painting
> over text. Drop the `box-shadow` gap … put the bar under the glyphs with `z-index: -1` on
> `.chunk-removal::after` …
>
> #244: … paint the bar under the text: `isolation: isolate` on `.cm-editor .cm-line` and
> `z-index: -1` on `.chunk-removal::after`, so glyph ink draws over the gap and the bar.

**Decision:** paint the bar **above the line's band but below the text**.

- Use `isolation: isolate` on `.cm-editor .cm-line` and `z-index: -1` on `.chunk-removal::after`.
  Inside each line, the line's own band background paints first, then the bar, then the text and
  the word-mark backgrounds.
  - With `z-index: -1` alone, the bar would sit under the line's band too and be dimmed by it;
    report what you see if you try it.
- Drop the 1 px gap if, once the bar is under the glyphs, it no longer helps against an adjacent
  word mark. Keep it only if the 1x screenshots show it still separates the bar from the mark.
- Check that `isolation` on lines changes nothing else you can see. Compare the selection over
  marks, the active-line highlight and the bands with round 1's screenshots.
