## Contract (fixed; you may add, never change)

- `main.ts`: one exhaustive `switch` over the window kind — in the app `dataset["window"]` is `"merge" | "compare" |
  "range"`, anything else merge; in fixture mode `?range[=<case>]` → range, then `?diff` → compare, else merge.
  `startRange(theme)` creates the source, `createRangeStore`, `startUp`, mounts `RangeApp`: no `LanguageServers`, no
  merge store, no compare store.
- `ui/rangeStore.ts`: `createRangeStore(source: RangeSource, startTheme)` / `useRangeStore()`; state `start` (a mirror
  of `openRange`'s reply), `shown: RangeFileId | null` (written only by `showFile` and `startUp`), `visited` (first-visit
  order, at most `MOUNTED_FILES = 16`, the least recently shown dropped), `problem`, `screen`, the settings part.
- Fence PM9: `ui/Range*.vue`, `ui/range*.ts`, `ui/useRange*.ts` never import `ui/store.ts`, `ui/App.vue`,
  `ui/compareStore.ts`, `ui/CompareApp.vue` or `lsp/`; PM7 also forbids the compare root from importing the range
  root's files.
- app-session: `start-app --range <r> --repo <dir>` (`--range` needs `--repo`), `start-fixture --range-case <case>`.
