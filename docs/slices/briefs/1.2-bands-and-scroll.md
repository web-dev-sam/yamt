# Slice 1.2 — bands between the panes and locked scrolling (the novel part)

The three panes now show the engine's change blocks in colour. This slice adds what makes it
PhpStorm's merge window: curved bands in the two gaps that join each change block to its
counterpart in the next pane, and scrolling that keeps the three panes in step. After this
slice the owner compares the 10 real conflicts against WebStorm by eye (plan §2 #28: the
window, not the tests, judges the chunk algorithm). Nothing is clickable yet.

## Contract (fixed; you may add, never change)

`connectors/index.ts` — pure geometry, numbers in, SVG path strings out, no DOM:

```ts
export type Span = { readonly top: number; readonly bottom: number }; // px from the strip's top; top === bottom is an insertion point
export type Band = { readonly left: Span; readonly right: Span };
export function bandPath(band: Band, width: number): string;
```

`bandPath` is one closed path: the top edge is a cubic curve from `(0, left.top)` to
`(width, right.top)` with both control points at `x = width / 2` (one at each end's height), a
straight right edge down to `right.bottom`, the bottom edge the same kind of curve back to
`(0, left.bottom)`, then close. An insertion point on one side makes the band taper to a line on
that side. Number formatting is yours; tests pin exact strings.

`editor/`:

- `MergeHosts` gains `strips: readonly [SVGSVGElement, SVGSVGElement]` (left gap, right gap;
  architecture §5.2). `MergeView.vue` passes its two `<svg>` elements through
  `useMergeController` (template refs, VU4).
- Left gap: one band per chunk whose local side changed (the same rule that decides the left
  pane's colours — today private in `editor/internal/decorations.ts`; share it, do not copy it),
  joining its `local` lines in the left pane to its current range in the center (`chunkField`).
  Right gap: chunks whose remote side changed, joining the center range to the `remote` lines.
  A band takes the tone of its side (`chunkTone(chunk, "local")` / `"remote"`), filled from the
  same `--color-chunk-*` tokens as the line colours (VU14), so a band and the lines it joins read
  as one shape.
- Band edges line up with the pane line edges on screen (±1 px) at any scroll position. Only
  bands whose span intersects either pane's visible area are drawn (CM11).
- Repaint after any pane scroll, any center edit, and any layout change (window resize, font
  load), at most once per animation frame, with no layout reads inside scroll handlers (CM13).
- Locked scrolling (plan §2 #12, CM12): scrolling any pane moves the other two so that the line
  at the vertical middle of the scrolled pane lines up with its counterpart in the others.
  Between chunks the panes move together line for line; across a chunk whose sides have
  different heights the followers move proportionally, and across an insertion point a follower
  holds still while the other side scrolls through the inserted lines. The mapping is one pure
  function over anchor pairs (architecture §6: `(0,0)`, each chunk's top and bottom, the content
  ends), so a later "hold at chunk" variant replaces only it (decision 12). Each pane can still
  be scrolled to its own first and last line. No feedback loop: a scroll the sync itself caused
  never triggers another sync (`syncing` flag cleared in the next frame).
- `destroy()` also removes every listener and observer and empties both `<svg>`s (CM14).

## Tests

`node` project (no layout):

- `connectors/`: exact path strings for an aligned band, a band whose right side is lower, an
  insertion on the left, an insertion on the right.
- scroll mapping (pure function): no chunks ⇒ identity; a chunk of 5 lines on one side and 12 on
  the other maps its top and bottom exactly and its interior proportionally; an insertion holds
  the follower still; positions beyond the last anchor; a synthetic 2,000-line file with 40
  chunks of mixed sizes at 20 px per line maps every chunk top exactly and is non-decreasing.
- any other pure helper you extract (e.g. line range → `Span`), at the line-space edges: empty
  range, range at the document end.

`editor/` is outside the mutation run: break each tested rule by hand once (e.g. control points
at `width / 3`; drop the insertion hold), watch the test fail, restore, report the failure line.
