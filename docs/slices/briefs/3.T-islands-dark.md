## Shapes (what the user sees)

WebStorm's island geometry: `Island.arc` 20 → 10 px corner radius; `Island.borderWidth` 6 → 5 px
between panels (the theme docs: the visible distance is 1 px less than the value); `Banner.arc`
12 → 6 px. Panels have no visible outline (`Island.borderColor` = the panel colour).

- The page is the frame colour. Two panels sit on it, each 10 px rounded, 5 px apart and 5 px from
  the window's edges:
  - the file list (header "Files" and rows), full window height; no line on its right edge any more;
    its width handle sits in the 5 px gap and still drags as today;
  - the open file's view below its toolbar: the header row (side names, Keep, "Result" + Reset) and
    the three panes with the two strips between them form one panel; the header row keeps its
    bottom line, the strips keep their side lines, both in `--color-separator`. The choice view
    (files the editor cannot show) is the same kind of panel, its column lines kept.
- On the frame, not inside a panel: the toolbar (same height and content as today, no bottom line),
  the empty bar that stands in for the toolbar on read-only files and in the choice view (same
  height, so switching files never jumps), and the notice / problem / "All files are resolved" bars,
  each a 6 px rounded box with a 1 px border in its border colour, 5 px from its neighbours.
- File list rows: hover `--color-row-hover`, selected `--color-row-selected`; buttons keep their
  4 px corners (`Button.arc` 8), outlined ones in `--color-control-border`, the filled one in
  `--color-primary`; icon buttons hover `--color-hover`.
- The question dialog: `--color-panel` background, `--color-control-border` border (it floats over
  panels of its own colour), corners as today.
- The find bar: `--color-panel-inline` background, `--color-separator` border (it is drawn with
  CodeMirror's `.cm-panels`, today on `--color-window`).
- The panes: `--color-panel` background and gutter, active line `--color-active-line`; text, line
  numbers, selection, caret, change colours, bands and arrows look as today apart from the darker
  ground under the translucent change colours.

## Constraints

- Plan §2 #51: scrolling must stay as smooth as today. Rounded corners that clip their content
  (`overflow: hidden` + radius) around the scrolling panes or the strips' compositing layers
  (`will-change: transform` wrappers) can force WebKitGTK into extra mask layers. Measure before and
  after (Smoke 2); if the clipped panel is slower, shape the corners without clipping the scroller
  (for example: round only the panel's own background and keep the pane edges inside the radius)
  and say what you chose in `deviations_from_brief`.
- `just check`'s palette test (`frontend/scripts/palette.test.ts`) must pass unchanged: no colour
  literal outside `style.css`'s `@theme`, no unknown token.
- No new permanent test: the palette test already catches a missing or unknown token; colour values
  are proven by the smoke screenshots. Update an existing test only if it pins a class or token this
  brief renames.
