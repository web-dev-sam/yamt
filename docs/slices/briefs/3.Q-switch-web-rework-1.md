# Slice 3.Q-switch-web — fix round 1 (`ServerSwitchWebR1`)

Board "Servers and panes" row 5. Round 1 is commit `e62d468` (brief `briefs/3.Q-switch-web.md`, notes
`.omp/run/notes/ServerSwitchWeb.md`). One promoted finding from the boundary review, verbatim:

> **#1277** (promoted, boundary) `frontend/src/ui/LanguageServerSettings.vue:43` — Two places set whether the switch
> shows checked: Vue's `:checked="!off.has(spec.key)"` and `flip`'s `input.checked = !off.value.has(spec.key)` after the
> await. The `event.target instanceof HTMLInputElement` check exists only to make that second write, and it is why a
> second click during a write reads an old `off` (open risk 1). As the app grows, the next Settings control that writes
> through the store will likely copy this pattern.
> Fix: replace `flip` with `@click.prevent="switchServer(spec.key, off.has(spec.key))"`. A cancelled click on a checkbox
> puts its checked state back (HTML activation behaviour), so `:checked` becomes the only writer, both after a reply and
> after a failed write.
