- yamt's locked scroll: `editor/internal/scrollSync.ts` (`ScrollSync`: scroll listeners set the source, `sync` writes
  the followers once per frame from `Frame`'s anchors, one-frame echo window); `Frame` (`frame.ts`) owns it. CodeMirror
  6.43 handles scroll in its DOM observer and measure cycle (`@codemirror/view` `dist/index.js`: `onScroll`,
  `onScrollChanged`, `measure`, `scrollAnchorPos`/`scrollAnchorHeight`, `ViewState.measure`, the viewport margin).
  The design claim "the height map is exact" (`.omp/run/notes/MinimapDesign.md`) may be the broken assumption — check.
- The earlier jump of #80 hit the same family: #1216 (a scripted scroll to a side's end settles elsewhere in MiniBrowser),
  #1219 (a jump to a change at a side's very end landed wrong twice).
- `pnpm -C frontend test:browser` cannot launch here (#1242); `editor/` is not mutation-tested.
