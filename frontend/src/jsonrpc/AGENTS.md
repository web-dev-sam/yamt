Owner skill: `skill://ts-conventions`. One JSON-RPC 2.0 connection over a text channel (plan §2 #70, #109), shared by `lsp/` (language servers) and the window's agent client: `Connection` sends requests and notifications through `ConnectionHandlers.send`, parses every incoming text once (`messages.ts`, valibot; a malformed one is logged and dropped), settles each request with a `Reply` (result, error with `code`/`message`/`data`, timed out, closed) and answers the other side's requests with the handler's `Answer`. `request` has a deadline and on it sends `ConnectionOptions.cancelNotification` when there is one (`"$/cancelRequest"` for language servers, `null` for the agent, which cancels a prompt by its own notification); `requestUntilAnswered` has none and settles on its answer or `close()`. `timers.ts` is the injectable clock. Framework-free (PM3) and imports no other `src/` module (`scripts/fences.ts`); the public surface is `index.ts`.
