`dueDate`: `CalendarDay` (month 1–12) made by `calendarDayFrom` from `"YYYY-MM-DD"` naming a real day, written back by `calendarDayText` (the stored form); `typedDay` reads what the user types, day first only (`3.11.2026`, `03.11.2026`, `03/11/2026`, `03-11-2026` with one separator throughout, or eight digits `03112026`; a four-digit year; a day that does not exist is refused, `11/3/2026` is 11 March); `compareDays` gives the whole days from one day to another (negative when the first is earlier); `localDay` is the local calendar day of a time; `daysLeft` is `compareDays` from `localDay` of now (both days at UTC midnight, so midnight, month ends and daylight-saving days count once); `daysLeftText` "Due today", "1 day left", "n days left", "1 day overdue", "n days overdue"; `dueDateText` is every shown date, `DD.MM.YYYY` with leading zeros ("03.11.2026"), no locale; `dateTimeText` is every shown time, that local date and the 24-hour local time ("30.10.2026, 15:04", the row's created-at hover).

Job log (request `docs/slices/request-ci-job-log.md` rules 4–6, design `docs/slices/briefs/ci-job-log-design.md` §1.2, §1.7–1.9, plan §2 #159): `jobLogView(raw)` turns the raw `/trace` text into `LogLine`s of styled `LogRun`s as GitLab's job page shows it (`lib/gitlab/ci/ansi2json/converter.rb`, `parser.rb`, `style.rb`, `line.rb`; bold from `ansi2html.rb`), in one scan: a break is `\r*\n`; the 32-character timestamp prefix is stripped from every line when the first line has it, and a line whose prefix ends in `+` continues the one before (only the `\n` goes, a `\r` before it then clears); `section_start`/`section_end` markers vanish and end a line that has text; a lone `\r` clears the line; only `ESC[…m` (SGR) is applied, by GitLab's exact-text code table (`sgr.ts` `penAfter`: `01` is nothing, `38;5;n` an indexed colour, `38;2;…` no truecolor, its numbers read as codes); every other escape, and C0 controls but tab, vanish; an escape cut off at a line's end drops the rest; 30–37 show bright while bold; the style carries across lines; a line without text is not shown; neighbouring text in equal styles is one run. Run text is text, never markup (RK57). `text` is the shown lines joined by `"\n"`: what the page's `search/` matcher runs over; `hitPlaces` maps its `{ line, from, to }` hits onto runs (a `from` at a run's end starts the next run, a `to` there ends in that run; a hit outside the view is left out). `basicColour` turns a 256-colour index into one of the 16 `AnsiColour`s, 16–255 the nearest on xterm's default palette (the triples classify, never paint: the page maps `AnsiColour` to the `--color-ansi-*` tokens). `jobLogSearch`: `firstHit` the last match, `steppedHit` wraps both ways, `hitCountText` "3 of 17", `NO_RESULTS`; for the page's highlights only the lines near the view count: `firstLineBelow(count, edge, y)` the first line whose growing `edge` is past `y` (binary search), `placesOnLines(places, first, end)` the places on lines `first` to `end` exclusive (binary search on the line order `hitPlaces` gives).
