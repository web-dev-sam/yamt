Words come only from `mergeRequestsText.ts` (sentences, labels, titles, the counts through `serverText.ts` `counted`) and `mergeRequests/` (filter names, choices, summary, `ciStatusText` for the icon's name and the box), except the buttons shared with other pages: Back (`serverText.ts` `BACK`), Retry (`aiText.ts` `RETRY`), Dismiss (`searchText.ts` `DISMISS_LABEL`).

Merge-request conflicts (plan §2 #138–#143): `MergeRequestRow.vue` shows the change size in its meta line (`sizeText`, title `sizeTitle`; none when GitLab gave none), `MergeRequestReadiness.vue` (a `w-7` column right of the CI box: the Lucide icon of `readinessIcon`, title and accessible name `readinessText`) and, left of the open-changes button, the row's one action from `mergeRequests/` `rowAction` (Resolve `git-merge`, Continue `step-forward`; a disabled one through the `<span :title>` hover of `OperationButtons.vue`; the spinner while it runs) with its reply line under the row (`rowReplyText`, `role="status"`, the error colour only when `refused`). `mergeRequestActions.ts` `createRowActions` (held by the slice) is the only writer of `actions` (one `RowActing` per row, replaced by the next click; a click while the row runs sends nothing) and `localMerge` (rules 8, 9): it is read at `show()`, on the window's `focus` while the page is shown (`MergeRequestsView.vue`) and after every resolve or continue reply; a failed read keeps what was known and reports the `localMerge` step. Its `setRow` (a new map with the row's value, or without the row for `null`) and `claimRow` (the row set busy unless it already is) are the one per-row write and busy guard of `actions` and of the slice's `openings`.

The merge window of a merge request (plan §2 #138–#143): `question.ts` is what is asked; `questionText.ts` is its words (`cancel: null` draws one button; a bullet list is kind `lines`). Abort of a merge yamt started (`mergeRequest`, written only by `listing.ts`) asks by returnTo/branchBefore, then shows the one-button notice if git left anything (`leftoverText.ts`, the page's `gitRefused` line uses it too), then `closeEndedMerge`. `pushing.ts` offers the push after Continue answers ReadyToPush: only Push pushes (RK45), a failure asks pushFailed again, Keep Local or Escape closes once. A failed push is the `push` problem ("Could not push <branch>"); a failed `closeEndedMerge`, after the push offer or after Abort's leftovers, is the `closeMerge` problem of the merge read before the action began ("Could not close the merge of <target> into <branch>"), never Abort's. Once Continue answers ReadyToPush, or Abort answers leftovers, git stays running: the window is ended for every reader.
