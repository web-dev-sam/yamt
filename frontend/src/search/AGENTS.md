Owner skill: `skill://ts-conventions`. The window-wide search's pure core (plan §2 #127, request `docs/slices/request-search.md` rules 5, 7, 9, 10, 12): one matcher per query (`matcherOf`: the text escaped into one `giu` RegExp, `gu` with Match Case; UTF-16 columns; lines split at `\n`, a trailing `\r` left out, never a cross-line match; `limit` hits per pane), the place rule and order (`filePlaces`: a hit inside change `k` groups with the other panes' hits of the same line text and column, identical lines first-with-first; a hit outside every change groups by its aligned lead line and column; rows in the lead pane's order, a side-only row at its change's lead start before that line's lead rows), each place's `PlaceKey` (panes, column, line text, ordinal — never a line number) and `Preview` (26 characters before the match, leading whitespace trimmed, `…` when cut), the results (`emptyResults` for a full run, `withFile` per file: dismissals kept while their place still matches, the `MAX_ROWS` 20,000 cap re-applied by rank), the dismissals (`dismissPlace`, `dismissFile`: a group's kept across one-file re-runs, cleared by a full run) and what the list shows (`listRows`: pane filters and closed groups, nothing dismissed back; `summaryOf`; `goToPane`: the lead pane when on, else the leftmost on pane).

`index.ts` is the only surface. Framework-free (PM3) and self-contained: nothing here imports another `src/` module, as `jsonrpc/` (PM2, `frontend/scripts/fences.ts`). Pure: no DOM, no timers, no state; the window's `ui/` owns running, pausing and the texts.

`internal/`: `match` (the matcher, `paneHits`), `preview`, `places` (the place rule, order and key), `results` (results, dismissals, the cap), `list` (rows, summary, go-to pane). Every file has its `*.test.ts` (node), each test named for the bug it catches.
