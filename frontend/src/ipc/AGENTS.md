Owner skills: `skill://ts-conventions`, `skill://ipc-contract`. `bindings.ts` is generated — never edit. `domain.ts` is the only place brands are created (the compare window's guards and `nextColumnVersion` included); `fixture.ts` is the only file allowed `as` (after valibot); `log.ts` is the only file allowed `console.*`. Fixture mode's git operation lives in `fixtureOperations.ts` (`?operation=`, `?rewords`, `?empty`); `createFixtureSource` takes the page's search as a required parameter. The compare window (`yamt --diff`, plan §2 #93–#97, #101) has its own `CompareSource` (`CompareSource.ts`): domain shapes and their conversions in `compareDomain.ts`, the app's in `tauriCompare.ts`, fixture mode's in memory in `fixtureCompare.ts` (`?diff` starts one empty "Compare 1", `?diff=<case>` loads `fixtures/compare/<case>.json` into tab 1; the tab rules mirror `crates/compare`).

The range window (`yamt --diff=<range>`, plan §2 #103–#107) has its own `RangeSource` (`RangeSource.ts`, read-only: `openRange`, `loadRangeFile`): the `RangeFileId` brand in `domain.ts`, camelCase shapes and their conversions in `rangeDomain.ts` (a texts reply's differences through `compareDomain.ts` `toDifferences`), the app's in `tauriRange.ts`, fixture mode's in `fixtureRange.ts` (`fixtures/range/<case>.json` = `{ start, files }`, a file's id its index; `?range` loads `review`, `?range=<case>` that case, an unknown case logs `range.fixture_miss` and lists nothing).

The range window's threads and GitLab (plan §2 #116–#120) come through `ReviewSource` (`ReviewSource.ts`: `listThreads`, `addThread`, `addNote`, `queueMessage`, `unqueueMessage`, `checkGitLab`, `sendQueue` with a progress callback over a `Channel`), which `RangeSource` extends: the `ThreadId`/`MessageId` brands in `domain.ts`, `toThreadLine`/`fromThreadLine` in `threadLine.ts`, camelCase shapes and their conversions in `reviewDomain.ts`, the app's in `tauriReview.ts`, fixture mode's in memory in `fixtureReview.ts` (`?threads=seeded`: one thread of each line kind on the review case's `src/board/board.ts` with a queued, a refused, a pending and an `aiAnswer` message; the store's refusals as Rust's: `EmptyMessage`, `UnknownRangeFile`, `UnknownThread`, `UnknownMessage`, `MessagePending`, `SendRunning`; each post after a 300 ms pause, progress per message) over `fixtureGitLab.ts` (`?gitlab=<script>`: `linedUp` by default, `checking` answers after 3 s, one not-lined-up answer per script, `refusal` refuses and `stopped` times out at the second post; an unknown script logs `review.fixture_miss`).

Your own notes until they are on GitLab (plan §2 #123): `editNote(message, text)` and `deleteMessage(message)` answer the new `ThreadList`. `fixtureReview.ts` mirrors Rust's order: `SendRunning` while its send runs, then `UnknownMessage`, `NotANote` (an AI answer), `MessagePending`, and for an edit `EmptyMessage`; an edit of a queued or refused note leaves it `queued` with the new text; a delete drops the note and a thread left empty; ids are never reused.

The GitLab merge-request overview (compare and range windows) comes through `MergeRequestSource` (`MergeRequestSource.ts`: `findGitLabProject`, `listMergeRequests` with a page callback, `openMergeRequest`, `openPage`), which `CompareSource` and `RangeSource` extend: the `MergeRequestIid`/`WebPage` brands in `domain.ts` (a `WebPage` is a failed job's or a merge request's https page, plan §2 #137), the shapes and their conversions in `mergeRequestDomain.ts` (`createdAt` to ms since 1970, a missing commit's `short_id` as `shortId`), the app's in `tauriMergeRequests.ts`: one `Channel<ListMessage>` per load carries every page, then exactly one end, and the load ends on that end message (or the command's IPC failure), never on the command's own answer, since Tauri may deliver a page after it; an unreadable page fails the load; no page reaches the callback after the load ended. Fixture mode's is `fixtureMergeRequests.ts` over `fixtureMergeRequestData.ts`: `?mr=<script>` (`list`, `slow`, `many`, `empty`, `noGlab`, `loggedOut`, `timeout`, `refused`, `pageFails`) scripts GitLab's answers, every row with its page `<origin>/-/merge_requests/<iid>`, and an opened page logs `mergeRequests.fixture_page`; without `mr` the project is `none`, and an unknown script logs `mergeRequests.fixture_miss` and is `none` too.

`tauri.ts` `readClipboard(selection)` reads the clipboard through the shell, never through WebKit (plan §2 #99): GTK's text request, `null` for unreadable or no text; `main.ts` hands it to `ui/linuxPaste.ts` `takeLinuxPastes` on Linux. The fence (PM8, `scripts/clipboardFence.ts`) is on WebKit's clipboard reads: `clipboardData` and `navigator.clipboard.read`/`readText` outside `ui/linuxPaste.ts`. `keys.ts` holds both platform tests: `isMac` (CodeMirror's `Mod`) and `isLinux` (the one Linux rule, also behind `showsAntialiasing`).

`themes.ts` (plan §2 #102) is the one table of the ten themes' group, button words and kind (`THEMES`, exhaustive over the generated `Theme`): `themeOf` reads `<html data-theme>` (anything not a name is yamt Dark), `themeKind` gives dark or light to the editor's dark flag and `color-scheme`, `themeRows` the Settings picker's five editors, each its dark then its light. Colours live only in the CSS blocks.
